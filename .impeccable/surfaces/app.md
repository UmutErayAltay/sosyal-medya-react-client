## Direction contract

THESIS: A working social app rendered as a lab notebook — the record of what
happened, not a feed styled to look impressive. Refuses the rounded-card,
purple-gradient SaaS default every AI-generated social clone ships.

OWN-WORLD: Warm paper ground (faint ruled horizontal lines), ink-black text,
one accent — a fountain-pen red-orange, reserved for the liked/active state
only. Structural chrome (timestamps, usernames-as-metadata, counts, nav) sets
in monospace; post content and headings set in a plain humanist sans. Entries
read as flush-left dated log lines — thin rule between them, no rounded
corners, no shadow, no card.

STORY: A visitor understands instantly this is a real log of real activity,
not a mockup. They register, watch their own entry land as a new line in the
record, like it (an ink-mark tick), read a profile as a page of entries.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Composer at top as an open notebook line. Below it, the feed
as flush-left dated entries: a small stamped-circle avatar, username +
timestamp in monospace on one line, content in sans beneath it, like/comment
counts as small ink-stamp marks at the entry's foot, a thin rule closing each
entry — no card boundary, the rule IS the boundary.

FORM: Field-notebook/lab-notebook world (assigned candidate 7 of 7 grounded
directions), raised with two donated disciplines: (1) one type size per role,
rank carried by weight/case/monospace-vs-sans rather than a size ladder —
donated by the declined timetable-slide-rack challenger; (2) counters tick
visibly on change instead of snapping instantly — donated by the declined
nixie-counter challenger. Seed key: e88f1ceb.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the
finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its
provenance.
